# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Home residents using a HarmonyOS phone, tablet, or 2-in-1 device to understand and control the state of living room and bedroom appliances at a glance.

## Product Purpose

SmartHome is a smart home control surface. It opens on a calm 3D central-control view, then lets users enter a room top view where each appliance is visible, status-aware, and directly controllable.

## Brand Personality

Minimal, calm, spatial. The interface should feel like a capable home command center, not a marketing page or a noisy device list.

## Anti-references

Avoid skeuomorphic smart-home panels, crowded dashboard tiles, neon cyberpunk control rooms, heavy purple-blue AI gradients, decorative glass cards, and appliance controls hidden behind long menus.

## Design Principles

Show the home before the controls.

Keep system state legible from the first screen.

Make every appliance feel physically located.

Prefer direct manipulation over nested settings.

Use motion and depth only when they clarify state or navigation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target readable contrast, large touch targets, clear selected states, and reduced-motion-friendly transitions. Status must not rely on color alone.
